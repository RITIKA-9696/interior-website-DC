Our Story

Founded on a Simple Idea: Rooms Should Fit Their People

Terra & Ochre began in 1998 as a two-person practice working out of a converted workshop in Berlin. What started as furniture restoration grew into full residential and commercial interior design — but the founding instinct never changed: listen first, decorate last.

Today the studio is a small, deliberately-sized team of architects, interior designers and project managers who still take on every project personally.
