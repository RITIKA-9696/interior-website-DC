Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

How long does a full residential project take?

+
Most single-home projects run 12–20 weeks from signed brief to handover, depending on scope and how much structural work is involved.

Do you work outside Berlin?

+
Yes — we regularly take on projects across Germany and occasionally further afield, with site visits scheduled around key milestones.

Can you work with an existing architect or contractor?

+
Absolutely. We regularly collaborate alongside existing project teams, joining at whichever stage makes sense.

What does a consultation cost?

+
Initial consultations are complimentary. A detailed scoping session is quoted once we understand the size of the project.
